Feature 14: Member Stats
Overview reference: extends Major Feature 7 (Leaderboard)

Summary
-------
A stats area that ranks league members across a few pick-quality categories, plus a per-member
stats profile. Members can see the leaders in each category and click any member to see that
member's numbers and their best and worst weeks.

User Stories
------------
- As a member, I can see who leads the league in each stat category.
- As a member, I can open any member's stats profile, including my own.
- As a member, I can see a member's best and worst weeks by correct picks and by points.
- As a member, I can see how often a member correctly picks underdogs that win.

Definitions
-----------
- Counted game: a game in the league's set for a week that is locked, has a final result, and
  is not voided (Feature 06). Pending, in-progress, and voided games never count in any stat.
- Counted pick: a member's pick on a counted game. A counted game the member did not pick
  counts as a miss for accuracy (the denominator is the number of counted games, not the number
  of picks made).
- Underdog: the team getting points per the line at lock (LockedGameSnapshot.SpreadAtLock).
  A game with no line at lock, or a spread of exactly 0, has no underdog and is excluded from
  the underdog stat.
- Underdog win: a counted game where the underdog won.

Stat Categories
---------------
1. Pick accuracy
   - Value: correct counted picks / counted games, shown as a percentage and the raw fraction
     (e.g., "68.4% (93/136)").
2. Underdog hits
   - Value: underdog wins the member picked / underdog wins in the league's counted games so far,
     shown as a percentage and the raw fraction (e.g., "41.7% (5/12)").
   - The denominator is the same for every member who was in the league for all of those weeks.
     A member who joined late only has the underdog wins from the weeks they were a member.
3. Best and worst weeks (profile only, not a leaderboard category)
   - Most correct week: the Complete week with the highest correct-pick count.
   - Least correct week: the Complete week with the lowest correct-pick count.
   - Most points week: the Complete week with the highest points total.
   - Least points week: the Complete week with the lowest points total.
   - Each shows the week number, the value, and that week's game count (e.g., "Week 6: 11/12").
   - Ties: show the most recent week and a "+N more" indicator listing the other tied weeks.
   - Only Complete weeks (Feature 06) count. An in-progress week is excluded until it is Complete.

Acceptance Criteria
-------------------
League stats page
- Given a league, then a Stats page lists each leaderboard category (Pick accuracy, Underdog
  hits) with members ranked by that category's value, highest first.
- Each category shows every current member with: rank, name, value, and raw fraction.
- Ties share a rank, using the same rule as the season leaderboard (Feature 07).
- The viewing member's row is highlighted.
- Every member name links to that member's stats profile.
- Before any game is counted, the page shows an empty state ("Stats appear after the first
  week's games go final").
- The page is reachable from the season leaderboard and the bottom navigation or More menu
  (placement to be settled in UI design).

Member stats profile
- Given a member of the league, when any member of the league opens their profile, then it shows:
  - Pick accuracy and Underdog hits, each with the member's league rank in that category.
  - Most correct, least correct, most points, and least points weeks.
  - A per-week table: week, correct/total, points, weekly rank. Each row links to that week's
    leaderboard drill-down (Feature 07).
- Members can only view stats profiles for members of leagues they belong to.
- Former members (removed from the league) do not appear on the league stats page. Their
  profile is reachable from past weekly leaderboards and shows stats for the weeks they played.

Freshness
- Stats update when a game goes final, is overridden, or is voided, in the same pass that
  updates scoring and the leaderboard. No separate refresh is needed.
- Stats show provisional values while a week is in progress, using the same "In Progress"
  labeling as the weekly leaderboard. Best and worst weeks use only Complete weeks.

Performance
- The league stats page and a member profile each load in under 1 second for a league of up
  to 50 members and 15 weeks.

Out of Scope (for now)
----------------------
- Streaks, accuracy by conference, points-over-time charts, and favorite-cover stats. These are
  natural follow-ups once the stats data is in place.
- Cross-season or career stats.

Open Questions
--------------
- Should a member need a minimum number of counted games to appear on a category leaderboard
  (e.g., a late joiner at 3/3 = 100% would lead accuracy)? Proposed: show them, sorted by
  value, with a minimum of one Complete week. Answer: No, all members currently have picked all available games
- Should Underdog hits rank by percentage or by raw count of underdog wins picked? Proposed:
  percentage, with the raw count as the tiebreaker. Answer: percentage, not points earned
