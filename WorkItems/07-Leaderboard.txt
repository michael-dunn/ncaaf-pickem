Feature 07: Leaderboard
Overview reference: Major Feature 7

Summary
-------
The leaderboard shows standings both for a single week and for the season to date. It updates as
games go final. Members can drill into any past week to see everyone's picks and results.

User Stories
------------
- As a member, I can see the season standings ranked by total points.
- As a member, I can see who won a specific week.
- As a member, I can see a past week's picks for every member alongside the results.
- As a member, I can see how many points I am behind the leader.

Acceptance Criteria
-------------------
Season leaderboard
- Given a league, then the season leaderboard lists every current member ranked by total points
  descending.
- Ties in total points share the same rank (e.g., two members tied at 1st, next member is 3rd).
- Each row shows: rank, member name, total points, points behind leader, and number of weekly wins.
- The viewing member's row is visually highlighted.
- Each row shows a trend indicator (up, down, or unchanged) comparing the member's season rank
  after the most recent Complete week to their rank after the week before it. Week 1 shows no
  trend.
- No season-end champion banner or past-season archive is required; the season leaderboard after
  the final week is the record.

Weekly leaderboard
- Given a week, then the weekly leaderboard lists every member ranked by that week's points.
- Each row shows: rank, member name, weekly points, correct picks (e.g., 9/12).
- The weekly winner(s) are marked with a trophy indicator; ties share the win.
- A week that is not yet Complete (Feature 06) is labeled "In Progress" and its standings are
  provisional.

Week drill-down
- Given a completed or in-progress week, when a member opens it, then they see a grid of games
  (rows) by members (columns) showing each pick, colored correct/incorrect/pending, with the game
  result.
- Voided games (Feature 06) are shown but greyed out.
- The grid is horizontally scrollable on mobile with the game column pinned.

Navigation
- Members can move between weeks with prev/next controls and jump to the current week.
- Only weeks that have had a game set generated are navigable.

Former members
- Members removed from the league appear in past weekly leaderboards for weeks they participated
  in, but not on the season leaderboard.

Performance
- The season leaderboard loads in under 1 second for a league of up to 50 members and 15 weeks.

Resolved Questions
------------------
- Should there be a season-end "champion" banner and archive of past seasons? A: season end banner is not needed. 
- Should the leaderboard show a trend (up/down arrows week over week)? A: Yes, each week should show up and down trends
