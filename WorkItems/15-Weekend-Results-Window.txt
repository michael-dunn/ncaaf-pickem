Feature 15: Weekend Results Window
Overview reference: amends Major Feature 7 (Leaderboard) and the landing rule (04-Domain-Algorithms.md section 12)

Summary
-------
From the end of Saturday's games through Monday night, the app should be about the week that
was just played. Today the season calendar rolls the "current week" to the next week at Sunday
00:00 ET, so "This week's leaderboard" opens an empty, unplayed week, and the landing screen
falls back to Picks after Sunday. This feature keeps the just-played week as the week members
see until picks open on Tuesday (Feature 16).

Current Behavior (for reference)
--------------------------------
- SeasonCalendar.WeekWindow: a week's window runs from the preceding Sunday 00:00 ET, so the
  current week flips to week N+1 at Sunday 00:00 ET.
- LeagueLandingRule: lands on the Leaderboard only on the opening day (Sunday) of week N+1, then
  Picks from Monday.
- Season.razor "This week's leaderboard" links to /weeks/{CurrentWeek}/leaderboard, which is
  week N+1 from Sunday on.

User Stories
------------
- As a member, on Sunday and Monday, I open the app and see the results of the week just played.
- As a member, when I tap "This week's leaderboard" on Sunday or Monday, I see the week just
  played, not next week's empty leaderboard.
- As a member, on Tuesday, the app opens on Picks for the new week.

Definitions
-----------
- Display week: the week the app treats as "this week" for navigation and landing. It is
  separate from the calendar's current week, which jobs still use.
- Picks-open moment for week N+1: defined in Feature 16 (Tuesday, Eastern).
- Results window: from week N's lock until week N+1's picks-open moment.

Acceptance Criteria
-------------------
Display week
- Given now is before week N+1's picks-open moment and week N's set is locked, then the
  display week is N.
- Given now is at or after week N+1's picks-open moment, then the display week is N+1.
- The API returns the display week to the client alongside the calendar's current week. All
  client "this week" links, the WeekNav "jump to current week" control, and the league home
  card use the display week.
- Server jobs (refresh, generation, lock, scoring) keep using the calendar's current week.
  This feature changes only what members see.

Landing screen
- Saturday from lock until week N is Complete: Dashboard (unchanged).
- Once week N is Complete, or from Sunday 00:00 ET whichever is first, until week N+1's
  picks-open moment: Leaderboard for week N. This includes all of Sunday and Monday.
- From week N+1's picks-open moment: Picks for week N+1.
- A league's first week still opens on Picks as soon as its picks are open (no previous week
  to show).
- After the league's last week: Leaderboard (unchanged).

"This week's leaderboard"
- Given the results window, when a member taps "This week's leaderboard", then week N's weekly
  leaderboard opens.
- Members can still navigate forward to week N+1 with WeekNav once its set exists. Before picks
  open it is shown per Feature 16 (not yet open).

Edge cases
- A week where a game runs late or is postponed past Saturday: the results window still runs to
  the picks-open moment; the week shows "In Progress" until Complete.
- Bye or gap weeks in a league's range: the display week is the most recent locked week until
  the next week's picks open.

Testing
- Domain tests for the display-week and landing rules covering: Saturday during games, Saturday
  after all final, Sunday 00:00, Monday 23:59, Tuesday picks-open moment, first league week,
  last league week, DST fall-back Sunday.
- Use the simulator (Phase 11) to walk a full Saturday to Tuesday cycle.
