Feature 13: Season Calendar and Current Week
Overview reference: implied by Features 01, 02, 04, 07 ("current week", "each week", "Saturday");
resolved questions on Eastern time and one-league-per-season

Summary
-------
Almost every feature refers to "the current week" and "Saturday" without defining them. This file
pins down how weeks are numbered, when one week becomes the next, what the season covers, and how
time zones are handled.

Definitions
-----------
- Season: a single calendar year of college football (e.g., 2026). A league belongs to exactly one
  season (Feature 01).
- Week: numbered as the data provider numbers it (Week 0 or 1 through the conference championship
  week, typically Week 14 or 15). Week numbers come from the schedule feed, not from counting
  Saturdays, so they stay aligned with rankings and schedules.
- Week window: a week runs from Sunday 12:00 AM ET through Saturday 11:59 PM ET. A game's week is
  the week the provider assigns it, which is what places delayed Saturday-night finishes in the
  correct week (Feature 06).
- Saturday game: a game whose scheduled kickoff, converted to Eastern time, falls on a Saturday.
  A Friday 11:30 PM Pacific kickoff is Saturday 2:30 AM Eastern and therefore counts as Saturday.
  This is the definition Feature 02 uses.
- Lock time: the earliest Saturday kickoff in the league's game set for the week (Feature 05).

Acceptance Criteria
-------------------
Current week
- Given the current time is within a week window (Sunday 12:00 AM ET to Saturday 11:59 PM ET),
  then that week is the current week for picks and the league home page.
- Given the current time is Sunday 12:00 AM ET or later, then the previous week is no longer
  pickable even if some of its games are still being scored (a delayed game is scored into the
  week it belongs to, but new picks are never accepted for it).
- The leaderboard "jump to current week" control (Feature 07) goes to this current week.

Season range
- Given a commissioner creating a league, then they choose the first and last week the league will
  play. Defaults are Week 1 through the final regular-season week. Week 0 is never included by
  default but the commissioner may select it as the first week.
- Weeks outside the league's range have no game set and are not pickable or navigable.
- Conference championship week, bowl games, and the College Football Playoff are out of scope.
  Only the regular season is played; the last selectable week is the final regular-season week.
- Given the current date is before the league's first week, then the league home shows "Season
  starts Week N on <date>" and the picks page is unavailable.
- Given the current date is after the league's last week's window, then the league is marked
  Complete and the season leaderboard is final.

Time zones
- All times are stored in UTC.
- All scheduling logic (week boundaries, Saturday determination, reminder times in Feature 11)
  evaluates in America/New_York, including daylight saving transitions.
- All times displayed to a member are rendered in the member's device time zone, with the zone
  abbreviation shown next to lock time so a Mountain-time member is not confused by an Eastern
  lock.

Schedule changes
- Given the schedule feed moves a game from Saturday to another day after the game set was
  generated but before lock, then the game is removed from the set and affected members are
  notified (Feature 11, "new games added" pattern in reverse: "a game was removed").
- Given the same change after lock, then the game is voided (Feature 06).

Resolved Questions
------------------
- Should the league default to including Week 0 when the season has one? (Proposed: no, default
  to Week 1, commissioner can change.) A: no
- Should conference championship week be included by default? Most conferences play Friday and
  Saturday, so the set would be small. (Proposed: yes, include; the Saturday filter handles it.) A: no, only regular season is in scope
